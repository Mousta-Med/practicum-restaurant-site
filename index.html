Our story

Cooked over olive wood since 2014

Olive & Ember started as a small family kitchen with one idea: cook the food of the Mediterranean the way it has always been cooked, slowly and over a real fire.

Every morning we light the oven with olive wood, bake our bread, and build the menu around what our farmers and fishermen bring in. Nothing is rushed, and everything is meant to be shared.

  • 12 years by the fire
  • 100% house-made bread & desserts
  • Local farmers & fishermen
